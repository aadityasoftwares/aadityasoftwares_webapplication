Responsible Disclosure

Vulnerability Disclosure Program (VDP) & Safe Harbor

We take the security of our platforms, digital products, and client repositories seriously. We welcome responsible security reports from white-hat researchers and the global cybersecurity community.

Safe Harbor Guarantee: If you conduct vulnerability research in good faith and adhere strictly to the rules outlined below, Aaditya Softwares will consider your activities authorized, will not initiate civil litigation, and will not contact law enforcement.
  • Reporting Channel: Email security reports directly to security@aadityasoftwares.in (or contact@aadityasoftwares.in with subject [SECURITY VULNERABILITY]).
  • Response SLA: We acknowledge receipt of vulnerability reports within 48 hours and provide regular updates until resolution.
  • Prohibited Activities: Do not attempt Denial of Service (DoS/DDoS), social engineering of employees, physical security attacks, or extraction of customer/client proprietary data.
