Scope & Governance

Scope & Application of this Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to Aaditya Softwares (referred to herein as "Aaditya Softwares", "we", "us", or "our"), having its registered development hub in Vadodara, Gujarat, India. This document regulates how we collect, store, process, protect, and share data across:

  • Our public digital portals, website (aadityasoftwares.in), and interactive showcase pages.
  • Client discovery sessions, technical consultations, project estimates, and contractual RFP exchanges.
  • Active software engineering engagements, custom web and mobile app development, cloud infrastructure deployments, and ongoing maintenance retainers.
  • Our proprietary digital products and internal SaaS platforms, including the StickyJi platform.
Data Controller & Data Processor Roles: For website visitors, Aaditya Softwares acts as the Data Controller. In client development projects where we process end-user data on your behalf, we act strictly as a Data Processor governed by a bilateral Data Processing Agreement (DPA).
02
Data Categories

Information We Collect

We practice data minimization: we collect only information that is strictly necessary to deliver high-quality engineering services, maintain infrastructure security, and fulfill commercial contracts.

Client Contact Data

Full names, corporate email addresses, phone numbers, job titles, and company names provided through contact forms, email inquiries, or discovery calls.

Technical Specifications

System architecture requirements, API documentation, database schemas, feature wireframes, budget ranges, and timeline requirements.

Automated Telemetry

Standard server logs, browser user-agent, operating system, IP address, referral sources, and page interaction timestamps collected for security and performance optimization.

04
Intellectual Property

Client IP & Non-Disclosure Safeguards

A fundamental tenet of Aaditya Softwares is absolute client intellectual property protection. Software assets, business logic, source code, and data models shared with us are treated with strict confidentiality:

Bilateral NDA Protection

We execute standard mutual non-disclosure agreements prior to reviewing proprietary business logic, schemas, or confidential codebases.

No Public AI Training

We strictly forbid the ingestion of client source code, architectural blueprints, or client proprietary datasets into public LLM training datasets.

Isolated Tenant Environments

Client repositories, Docker containers, and test staging pipelines run in isolated, access-restricted workspaces with multi-factor authentication (MFA).

06
Cross-Border Compliance

International Data Transfers

Because Aaditya Softwares serves clients across North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific, information may be transferred to and processed in India, where our engineering center is headquartered.

For transfers from the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we implement Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission, ensuring recipients uphold equivalent levels of data protection.

07
Security Architecture

Data Storage, Encryption & Security Controls

We enforce multi-tiered defense-in-depth security measures to protect data against unauthorized alteration, disclosure, destruction, or interception:

Cryptographic Safeguards
AES-256 & TLS 1.3

All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3. Persistent database volumes and storage buckets use AES-256 bit encryption.

Access Control
Role-Based Access (RBAC) & MFA

Strict principle of least privilege. Hardware security keys / authenticator app MFA is mandatory across all internal developer accounts.

Code Auditing
Automated SAST & Secret Scanning

Continuous vulnerability scanning and automated secret detection to prevent accidental credential leakage in repositories.

Endpoint Security
Encrypted Developer Workstations

Company-managed developer machines feature full-disk encryption (BitLocker/FileVault), automatic OS security patching, and remote wipe capabilities.

08
Lifecycle Management

Retention & Deletion Policy

We retain personal and project data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was gathered:

  • Active Engagements: Retained throughout the project lifecycle and the contractual warranty/maintenance period.
  • Post-Project Repositories: Staged repositories and test environments are permanently wiped or transferred within 60 days following formal handover and final sign-off, unless a maintenance retainer is active.
  • Diagnostic Logs: Web server diagnostic telemetry is automatically purged on a rolling 30-day retention window.
  • Statutory Financial Records: Invoices and tax documents are retained for seven (7) years in compliance with Indian corporate and GST laws.
09
Empowerment

Your Statutory Privacy Rights

Regardless of your geographical location, Aaditya Softwares affords you comprehensive control over your personal data:

Right to Access & Portability

Request an encrypted copy of all personal records we hold regarding you or your organization in a machine-readable JSON or CSV format.

Right to Rectification

Update or correct inaccurate or incomplete contact, organizational, or billing information at any time.

Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")

Request the complete, verifiable erasure of your personal data from our systems, subject to legal compliance requirements.

Right to Object & Restrict Processing

Opt out of non-essential communications, email newsletters, or restrict processing activities with immediate effect.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@aadityasoftwares.in. We respond to all verified statutory requests within 72 business hours with zero administrative fees.

10
Browser Storage

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use small text files called cookies to facilitate website navigation, maintain security sessions, and analyze aggregated traffic patterns:

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: Essential for site routing, CSRF token validation, and performance caching. Cannot be disabled.
  • Preference Cookies: Store theme or navigation preferences across visits.
  • Privacy-Preserving Analytics: Aggregated, anonymized statistics without cross-site tracking or advertising profile construction.

You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or indicate when a cookie is being sent via your browser settings.
